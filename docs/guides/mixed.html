Optional practice challenges

Silent statue

Meet the camera and microphone goals together.

  1. Stay still (15% or less) and quiet (−30 dBFS or lower). Hold for 12 consecutive seconds.
Open Silent statue in the studio

Sensor relay

Pass a stillness stage followed by a sound stage.

  1. Keep movement at or below 15%. Hold for 6 total seconds.
  2. Keep microphone input at or below −30 dBFS. Hold for 6 consecutive seconds.
Open Sensor relay in the studio

Quiet return

Leave the starting view, return, then settle quietly.

  1. Move a little to one side and hold the changed view. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  2. Stay near the starting camera view. Hold for 4 total seconds.
  3. Stay still (15% or less) and quiet (−30 dBFS or lower). Hold for 6 consecutive seconds.
Open Quiet return in the studio

Switchback

Move, speak softly, then freeze in silence.

  1. Make a small, visible movement each second (20% or higher). Hold for 3 total seconds.
  2. Hum or speak softly between −36 and −12 dBFS. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  3. Stay still (15% or less) and quiet (−30 dBFS or lower). Hold for 6 consecutive seconds.
Open Switchback in the studio

Full circuit

Complete all five movement, position and sound stages.

  1. Keep movement at or below 8%. Hold for 5 total seconds.
  2. Move a little to one side and hold the changed view. Hold for 4 total seconds.
  3. Stay near the starting camera view. Hold for 4 total seconds.
  4. Hum or speak softly between −36 and −12 dBFS. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  5. Stay still (15% or less) and quiet (−30 dBFS or lower). Hold for 8 consecutive seconds.
Open Full circuit in the studio

Quiet clamp

Meet the quiet and stillness limits at the same instant.

Strict course. Every reading must qualify. A miss, pause or gap resets the current hold.

  1. movement 0–4% and microphone at or below -44 dBFS. Hold for 12 consecutive seconds.
Open Quiet clamp in the studio

Voice in register

Hold your starting position while maintaining a soft sound band.

Strict course. Every reading must qualify. A miss, pause or gap resets the current hold.

  1. 0–1 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone -38 to -32 dBFS. Hold for 5 consecutive seconds.

Silent offset

Hold a small positional shift under the sound ceiling.

Strict course. Every reading must qualify. A miss, pause or gap resets the current hold.

  1. 3–8 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone at or below -44 dBFS. Hold for 10 consecutive seconds.
Open Silent offset in the studio

Triple lock

Match the starting scene, stay still, and hold deep quiet together.

Strict course. Every reading must qualify. A miss, pause or gap resets the current hold.

  1. movement 0–4% and 0–1 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone at or below -46 dBFS. Hold for 12 consecutive seconds.
Open Triple lock in the studio

Final inspection

Move through an offset hold, voiced return, quiet freeze and triple lock.

Strict course. Every reading must qualify. A miss, pause or gap resets the current hold.

  1. movement 0–4% and 3–8 camera tiles changed from the starting view. Hold for 5 consecutive seconds.
  2. 3–8 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone at or below -44 dBFS. Hold for 5 consecutive seconds.
  3. 0–1 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone -38 to -32 dBFS. Hold for 4 consecutive seconds.
  4. movement 0–4% and microphone at or below -44 dBFS. Hold for 6 consecutive seconds.
  5. movement 0–4% and 0–1 camera tiles changed from the starting view and microphone at or below -46 dBFS. Hold for 10 consecutive seconds.
