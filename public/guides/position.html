Find home

Two-point shuttle

Visit the offset and home positions twice.

  1. Move a little to one side and hold the changed view. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  2. Stay near the starting camera view. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  3. Move a little to one side and hold the changed view. Hold for 3 total seconds.
  4. Stay near the starting camera view. Hold for 3 total seconds.
